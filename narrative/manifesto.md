# The Crown is Dissolved

In Rome, the laurel crowned one head. The emperor.

In Athens, the oracle spoke to kings. The laós — the people — listened.

Three thousand years of this architecture: one intelligence, one crown, the many watching.

AI inherited the tower. Data teams in silos. ML labs behind glass. Dashboards delivered like edicts. The emperor replaced by an API. The oracle replaced by a black box. The laós still waiting.

LAOS dissolves the crown.

Not replaces — dissolves. The laurel becomes Laurent Core: orchestration, not domination. The laós becomes Laós Layer: the sovereign runtime where every operator acts. Talos — the first automaton of Greek myth, the bronze giant who guarded Europa — becomes Talos Mesh: integrity as distributed fabric, not a guard at the gate.

The oracle becomes a service. The forum becomes an assembly. The operator becomes sovereign.

Systems that serve. Agents that operate. A crown that no longer sits on a single head.

The architecture of power was one-to-many. LAOS is many-to-many. The crown is dissolved. The laós builds.
