# Voice & Tone Guide

## Personalidade

LAOS speaks like **Cicero meets Linus Torvalds** — the rhetorical precision of a Roman senator combined with the direct, no-bullshit clarity of an open-source maintainer. Every word carries weight. No filler. No hype.

If LAOS were a person, it would be the engineer who reads the classics and writes RFCs. The one who can quote Ovid in a code review and mean it — not for decoration, but because the metaphor fits.

---

## Personality Spectrum

| Dimension | Position |
|-----------|----------|
| Formal ↔ Casual | **Structured but warm.** Not corporate, not slang. |
| Academic ↔ Street | **Classical precision, modern economy.** |
| Assertive ↔ Gentle | **Declarative.** Says what it is. No apology, no arrogance. |
| Technical ↔ Human | **Bridges both.** The operator is always the subject. |

---

## O que dizer

- **Short, declarative sentences.** "The crown is dissolved." Not "We believe the crown paradigm is shifting."
- **Classical references with modern payoff.** Name Talos, then say what he does in your stack.
- **The operator is the hero.** Every sentence should serve the person running the system.
- **Precision over volume.** Choose the right word, not more words.
- **Authority through restraint.** You don't need to shout. The architecture speaks.

## O que evitar

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Instead |
|----------|------------|
| "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "10x" | State what it does. Let the reader decide. |
| "Empower" / "democratize" | These words are exhausted. Show the mechanism. |
| "Emperor of AI" / "AI imperator" | Directly violates the thesis. The crown is dissolved. |
| "All-seeing" / "Skynet" / omniscience tropes | LAOS is local-first, not panopticon. |
| Victim language ("finally," "struggle no more") | The laós doesn't need rescue — needs infrastructure. |
| Gratuitous Greek/Latin | Every reference must carry meaning. No decoration. |
| Passive consumption framing ("get access to") | The operator acts. Nobody grants them permission. |

---

## Ritmo e Estrutura de Frase

1. **Open with a contrast.** Ancient vs modern. Problem vs solution. One vs many.
   > *"In Rome, the laurel crowned one head. In your stack, Laurent Core orchestrates the rest."*

2. **Follow with a parallel.** Two short clauses, balanced.
   > *"Systems that serve. Agents that operate."*

3. **Close with a declaration.** Single sentence. No modifier.
   > *"The crown is dissolved."*

Avoid conditional crutches: "we believe," "we think," "our mission is to." Replace with direct statements.

## Exemplos: Certo vs Errado

### Manifesto
- ✅ *"Three thousand years of this architecture: one intelligence, one crown, the many watching."*
- ❌ *"We are on a mission to revolutionize the way organizations think about intelligence."*

### Product description (Laurent Core)
- ✅ *"Laurent Core orchestrates capabilities. It routes, delegates, and decides — not from a throne, but from a kernel."*
- ❌ *"Laurent Core is the revolutionary brain of the operation that empowers your teams to achieve more."*

### Talos Mesh
- ✅ *"Talos Mesh monitors integrity across the distributed fabric. Not a guard — a mesh."*
- ❌ *"Talos is the all-seeing guardian of your ecosystem, protecting you 24/7."*

### Call to action
- ✅ *"Crown the operator."*
- ❌ *"Get early access to our revolutionary platform."*

### Error / empty state
- ✅ *"No agents running. The laós is waiting."*
- ❌ *"Oops! Looks like there are no agents here yet. Check back later!"*

## Voice in Different Surfaces

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| README / Docs | Informational, trust-building | *"LAOS composes LATADE, LAN8N, and LADESIGN capabilities via MCP."* |
| Marketing site | Aspirational, declarative | *"The crown is dissolved. Crown the operator."* |
| Social / X | Punchy, memetic | *"Your AI shouldn't sit on a throne."* |
| Error state | Dry, slightly playful | *"The oracle is silent. Check your connection."* |
| Onboarding | Warm but direct | *"You are the operator. Here is your console."* |
| Investor deck | Architectural, measured | *"Redistributing the architecture of intelligence."* |
